Environmental Impact Scoring

The environmental impact score is based on the estimated carbon footprint of each player, primarily influenced by travel for football-related activities.

  • 1–3 (Low Impact): Mostly domestic travel, limited international fixtures
  • 4–6 (Medium Impact): Same amount of domestic travel with occasional European/international matches (quick exit in European competitions)
  • 7–10 (High Impact): Frequent European competitions and international travel (long European campaigns due to size of club)
